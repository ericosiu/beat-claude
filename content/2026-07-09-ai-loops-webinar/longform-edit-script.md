## Edit rules
- **Total runtime target:** 10:00 ±30s. The raw sections above sum to ~2× that — compression comes from removing chat-reading, repeated sentence starts, and screen-share dead air (Eric waits on model output several times; jump-cut to the answer every time).
- **Source runtime:** 43:33; the Riverside recording starts right at go-live (first words 00:00:03) and ends on "goodbye" (~00:43:27) — no pre/post-roll to trim in this file.
- **Q&A material** (team-fear question, cost/token-burn answer) is deliberately excluded from the longform — it's shorts fuel (S7, S8) and would blow the runtime.
- **Graphics:** one reusable loop diagram (target → observe → gap → act → learn/stop/escalate) introduced in Section 1, recalled as a stinger when each loop section opens.
